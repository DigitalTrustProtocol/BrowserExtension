# Chrome Web Store listing

The short description is the `description` field in `public/manifest.json`. It ships inside the extension package.

Paste the long description below into the dashboard **Store listing** detailed description. It is not read from the package.

## Long description

```text
Start letting your personal peers decide what deserves your attention.

Attention helps you trust people and rate posts on X, then see those judgments right where you read. A like is a crowd score. Attention is advice from people you actually trust.

On an account, you see whether your circle trusts them, and how that trust reaches you. On a post, you see a simple score: worth reading, or not worth your time, down to spam. Rate it yourself in one tap. Hide the posts that miss your bar, so your home feed shows less noise.

Your judgments are yours. You choose what to publish, and you can try the whole experience with sample data before you share anything. Attention does not post, like, follow, or message for you.

Spend your attention on people worth hearing, and on posts worth reading.

Privacy policy: https://digitaltrustprotocol.github.io/BrowserExtension/privacy.html
Contact: attention@trust.dance
```
